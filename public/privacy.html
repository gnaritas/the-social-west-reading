Privacy

Privacy

A bar website should not be a surveillance operation. Here’s everything this site does with your information.

What gets collected

Only what you type into the contact form: your name, optionally a phone number and email, and your message. It’s sent to the people who run The Social so they can answer you — opening news, questions, requests. It isn’t sold, and it isn’t added to some marketing machine behind your back.

Cookies and trackers

This site sets no cookies of its own, loads no third-party scripts, and shows no ads. The one exception is the map: the embedded Google map loads from Google, and Google may set cookies inside that map frame under its own policy.

Hosting

The site is hosted for The Social on standard, ad-free web hosting. Messages are delivered through an email provider that processes them to deliver them — nothing more.
