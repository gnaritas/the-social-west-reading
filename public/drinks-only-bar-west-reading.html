Drinks only

A drinks-only bar for West Reading

“Drinks only” isn’t marketing copy here — it’s the health inspection filing. The county report on 643 Penn Ave lists bar service with no food service, and The Social is being built to exactly that brief.

A bar the paperwork agrees on

No kitchen means no food menu, no grill tickets, no “we’ll bring it out when it’s ready.” Order a drink, settle in, and the only thing between you and another round is asking for it. Dinner happens somewhere else in the neighborhood — which, on Penn Avenue and the blocks around it, is not a hardship.

Who to trust with a drinks-only room

The Social comes from Erik Arizmendi — owner of Oakbrook Brewing, a West Reading brewery, and Taqueria Comalli, a West Reading taqueria. A brewery family opening a room with nothing but drinks on the bill is a bet the town can live with; the drinks program itself hasn’t been announced, and it’ll be posted here when it’s real.

By the numbers so far

  • Service: bar only — no food service (county health inspection report, via Berks County Eats, Nov 2026).
  • Address: 643 Penn Ave, West Reading, PA 19611.
  • Opening: projected December 2026 – January 2027.
  • Hours / drinks list / opening night: to be announced.
