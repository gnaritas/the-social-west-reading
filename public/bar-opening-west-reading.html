New bar opening

A new bar is opening in West Reading: The Social, 643 Penn Avenue

A new bar is coming to West Reading, Pennsylvania. The Social — a drinks-only bar from the team behind Oakbrook Brewing and Taqueria Comalli — takes over the former Willow Creek Brewing house, with a projected opening of December 2026 to January 2027.

The short version

  • What: The Social — a drinks-only bar (bar service only, per the county health-inspection report).
  • Where: 643 Penn Ave, West Reading, PA 19611 — the former Willow Creek Brewing house on Penn Avenue.
  • When: projected December 2026 to January 2027; opening night not yet announced.
  • Who: Erik Arizmendi — owner of Oakbrook Brewing and Taqueria Comalli, both West Reading businesses. The Social is the team’s first own-name bar.
  • Contact: TaqueriaComalli@gmail.com — the bar isn’t open yet, and this inbox is monitored.

Why the address matters

643 Penn Avenue is not a blank spot on a map — for years it was Willow Creek Brewing, one of the places West Reading built its Friday nights around. The new bar keeps the house and changes what happens inside it: no kitchen, no food service, just the drinking end of a night out, run by people this town already drinks with.

Following the opening

No hours, drinks list, or opening-night plans have been announced as of November 2026. This site is where the confirmed news lands, and emailed readers hear it first. Ask us to keep you posted.
