Three things we know today

Drinks, full stop

The health inspection filing for 643 Penn Ave says it plain: bar service only, no food service. No kitchen, no grill — a room built for staying with a good drink.

An address West Reading already knows

The Social takes over the Penn Avenue building years of Friday-night traffic knew as Willow Creek Brewing. Same old house, new purpose: a bar.

The people behind it

The Social comes from the team behind Oakbrook Brewing and Taqueria Comalli — led by Erik Arizmendi, owner of both, two spots that have spent years serving this town.

Still to be announced: hours · what’s on tap and behind the bar · opening night. When the team says it, it lands on this page. Easiest way to hear it first: drop us a note.

A drinking room, in a town with a drinking pedigree

West Reading does bars its own way: a brewery, a beloved taco counter, pub rooms where the bartender knows your round. The Social is pitched as that kind of regulars’ room — walk-up friendly, built for after-dinner and in-between, the kind of place you find out about from a friend.

Nothing about it is finished yet — that’s half the fun. The bones are a house West Reading already loves, and the people running it have spent years pouring beer and feeding neighborhoods a block or two away.

In the meantime, the team’s other spots are open: Oakbrook Brewing · Taqueria Comalli

Questions people ask

When does The Social open?

December 2026 to January 2027, as projected. An opening night hasn't been announced — when the team sets one, this page carries it.

Where is it?

643 Penn Ave, West Reading, PA 19611 — the old Willow Creek Brewing house. The map is right below this list.

Will The Social serve food?

No. The health inspection filing for 643 Penn Ave lists bar service only — no food service. There's no kitchen, so plan dinner around it; the neighborhood has plenty.

Who runs it?

Erik Arizmendi — owner of Oakbrook Brewing and Taqueria Comalli, both in West Reading. The Social is the same team's first bar of its own.

What will they pour?

Not announced yet. A bar from a brewing family — the specifics of the drinks list are still being settled, and we'll post them the day they exist.

How do I find out about opening night first?

Email TaqueriaComalli@gmail.com or drop a note through the contact form and ask to be told when the date is set — opening news goes out that way.
