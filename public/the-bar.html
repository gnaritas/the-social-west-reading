The Bar

Drinks, full stop.

This is the sentence every part of The Social is built around: the health inspection filing for 643 Penn Ave lists bar service only — no food service. Not a restaurant with a bar attached. A bar.

What “drinks only” actually means for a night out

Eat before, or send out for something once you’re parked at the room — but don’t come hunting for a kitchen, because there isn’t one. The whole visit is the drink order, the seat you settle into, and however long you feel like staying.

“Drinks only” isn’t a restriction — it’s a design decision, filed on paper with the county and backed by the people making it. A brewery family opening its own bar, in a house built for drinking, with no kitchen in the plans. That’s the concept, end to end.

What’s on the lists

Nothing announced yet — not the taps, not the cocktail program, not the prices. But this is a bar from a brewing family, opened in a town that already queues for their taps; expect the beer side to be taken seriously, and expect the rest to arrive as a done idea, not a guess. When the drinks list exists, it lands here first.

The room

The house is the old Willow Creek Brewing building on Penn Avenue — if you’ve spent a Friday night on that block in the last decade, you’ve already sat in it. What changes inside hasn’t been announced. What won’t change: it’s still the same familiar front door at 643.
