Where things stand

  1. Nov 2026 — the name is on the street. The Social shows up on the county’s most-read food column’s coming-soon list, and the health-inspection paperwork is already done: drinks only, no food service.
  2. Now — the in-between. The house is between lives: closed as Willow Creek Brewing, becoming a bar. Hours, drinks program, and opening night are all yet to be announced.
  3. Dec 2026 – Jan 2027 — the projected window. Openings in a former brewery space land when the work says so, not when a press release does. When a date is set, it’s posted here and emailed to everyone who asked.
